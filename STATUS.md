## Decision: free and open

The toolkit is released under GPL-3.0-or-later. It is built first as a working tool for the author's own measurements and published so others can use and inspect it. There is no commercial layer, so the GPL licence of the optional Galvani reader is compatible and no independent MPR decoder is planned.

## Validation on the author's own data (2026-09-10)

A Li metal half-cell cycled for more than 100 cycles on an MPG-2 (GCPL, EC-Lab 11.43). The measurement files are private and not in the repository, and their values are not quoted here.

- Coulombic efficiency agrees with EC-Lab's own `Efficiency/%` column to 4e-6 % for every cycle; discharge capacity agrees with EC-Lab's `Capacity/mA.h` to 6e-9 mAh (the residual is EC-Lab's single-precision display).
- The MPR binary and its 31 MB MPT export give identical results (2e-16 mAh).
- One interrupted final charge is correctly reported as unpaired, and flagged by the diagnostic for a branch passing more than three times the median charge.

## Cyclara (0.6.0, 2026-10-19)

A desktop app on the cycling engine (PySide6 6.12.0 and pyqtgraph 0.14.0, the stack of ADR-005), sharing one workflow layer (`electrochem/workflow.py`) with the command line. Design, decisions and verification: `docs/U02_Cyclara.md`.

- **Workflow.** Drop or open a file; a reading screen states only what has happened; four questions only the researcher can answer (charging direction; cycle order, when the file starts with a discharge; what the potential column measures; active-material mass or "No mass"); eight views; a cycle strip that is selector, legend and fade picture at once; export of journal-sized figures, every table, `settings.json` and an Origin folder to a new folder, never overwriting.
- **Figures.** Journal presets from the publishers' pages (Nature, Elsevier), exact widths, type 5 to 7 pt with sub- and superscripts raised to the journal minimum, panel letters 8 pt bold lowercase, legends placed where they cover nothing, and twelve checks after drawing, stored with every export.
- **Name, mark and motion.** Launch sequence, reading screen, view transitions, confirmations and a theme switch that spreads from the switch as a widening circle. All short, skippable, and reduced or off by the system's reduce-motion setting or View > Motion.
- **Windows and macOS.** Same physical type size on both, native menu bar on macOS, Desktop and Start-menu shortcuts or a `Cyclara.app` built by the setup scripts, the taskbar shows Cyclara's icon.
- **Small screens.** Works from 1024 × 600 points (a 14-inch laptop at 150 % scaling): the Overview arranges its panels for the room, axis titles shorten instead of being cut, the tables fold to their tab row (Ctrl+J), and the window opens at a size that fits the screen and reopens where it was left.
- **Independent review.** A separate agent reported 31 findings, six of them wrong results (areal labels over mAh values, efficiency points cut off the axis, cycle order never asked, current reversals netted without a step column, three-electrode files on the cell voltage, an infinite mass accepted). All 31 are fixed, each with a regression test: `docs/reviews/Cyclara_Review_2026-10-19.md`.
- **Synthetic demonstration file** (`data/demo/`, made by `scripts/make_demo_data.py`; its header and name say it is synthetic): 50 cycles with a known per-cycle answer, used by an end-to-end test (agreement to 1e-9 relative), the screenshots and the handout, so no picture shows private data.
- **Engine changes**: dQ/dE grid kept inside the measured potentials (peak prominences and widths of the upper charge peaks change; peak potentials do not); without a step column, branches end where the current changes sign; the potential column follows the configuration answer; one capacity basis for every label and value.
- **Found and worked around in pyqtgraph 0.14**: tick labels silently multiplied by 1000 after `enableAutoSIPrefix(False)`; an axis title not re-centred when its text changes.

## Other cyclers and one-click installers (0.7.0, 2026-10-20)

- **P08, implemented and tested.** `electrochem/readers/`: a typed import profile whose four conventions (current sign, direction source, capacity semantics, cycle base) have no defaults and need a line of evidence; a generic reader for delimited text and spreadsheets that collects every finding before it refuses (45 named failure modes, `docs/P08a_Import_Failure_Mode_Register.md`), measures the capacity semantics on the file and compares the instrument's counter with the integral of the current, charge and discharge separately; a proposer that maps an unknown header and asks only what no file states; a registry that remembers a confirmed instrument by its header fingerprint; and Cyclara's import review page. EC-Lab files are recognised by signature and keep the validated path untouched; readers and engine never import each other (a test enforces it). Specification: `docs/P08_Multi_Instrument_Import_Specification.md`. Which instruments are verified on real files: `docs/P08d_Instrument_Compatibility_Matrix.md` (P08d, 20 October 2026: 16 built-in profiles, each checked against a named real export; 21 public instrument files under `data/public/<vendor>/` re-checked independently by `tests/test_readers_real_files.py`; the matrix marks which dialects are verified on a shipped file, which on inspected files only, and which are recognised only). Binary formats other than `.mpr` are refused with an instruction to export text.
- **Export variants (P08e).** The same instrument exported with other columns, another order, other units or another sheet name is the same instrument: recognition rests on the time, current and voltage columns alone, the header's unit is read rather than frozen, learned profiles claim later exports by the same rule, and a renamed required column reopens the review page pre-filled from the saved profile. Verified on re-exports of the shipped Arbin and Landt files (`tests/test_readers_variants.py`).
- **Folder opening** screens text files cheaply (`looks_tabular`): notes and logs are left alone, every measurement is opened.
- **Installers.** `installer/build.py` builds `Cyclara-0.7.0-Setup.exe` (NSIS 3, per user, no administrator prompt) and `Cyclara-0.7.0-macOS-{AppleSilicon,Intel}.zip` from a checksummed CPython 3.12.15 (python-build-standalone 20261013), the pinned wheels for each platform and the toolkit; `installer/README.md`. Verified on the build machine: checksums, the private-data scan, PE and Mach-O import closure, byte-code and pure-Python import under a real 3.12, and the Windows build run end to end under Wine (the real binaries, every view of the demonstration file). Not verified: a Windows PC or a Mac in person; neither installer is code-signed.
- **Origin from any cycler.** The Origin folder (tables in Origin's import layout, the graph spec, the build script) is written from the engine's results, so it is the same for an Arbin, Neware or Landt file as for an EC-Lab one (tested). Help > Set up the Origin link installs OriginLab's originpro bridge into Cyclara's own Python once (Windows, Origin 2021+); the export message then offers Build Origin project, or the set-up when the bridge is absent.
- **In the app.** Help > Check this installation (the self-tests, run by the installed interpreter, reported in words); Help > Open the demonstration file and a *Try the demonstration* button; a log file for windowed launches (`platform_support.keep_a_log`), opened by Help > Show the error log; the home page and About no longer say EC-Lab only.

## Plateaus, large files, methods and citation (0.8.0, 20 October 2026, later the same day)

- **Several plateaus (P14, implemented and tested; not experimentally validated).** For every cycle, each dQ/dE peak gets the capacity under it, its partner in the opposite branch (paired within 200 mV, `peak_pair_window_mV`) or the status *unpaired: no counterpart within 200 mV*, and its presence across cycles (*first cycle only*, *appears from cycle k*). The branch's average potential stays energy / charge, the capacity-weighted mean of the plateau potentials. New outputs: `dqde_peaks.csv` (every cycle, 19 new columns), `dqde_pairs.csv`, `dqde_map.csv`, `summary.json` → `plateaus`, `fig6_dqde_map`, `fig7_dqde_surface` (3D), the Evolution view, the inspector's plateau block. Verified on a synthetic cell with plateaus known by construction (`tests/plateau_cell.py`): M01 sections 9 and 10, `docs/handoffs/P14_Plateaus_and_Evolution_Handoff.md`.
- **Large files (P08f).** EC-Lab text and `.mpr` through numpy fast paths, identical to the strict P03 importers on every public file (a 1 000 000-row export: 2.7 s instead of 33 s and 2.1 GB); vectorised segmentation (2 000 000 rows: 0.17 s instead of 8 s); other cyclers' exports streamed with progress and a 2 GiB limit, checked against a frozen reference of every public file. `docs/handoffs/P08f_Large_Files_Handoff.md`.
- **In the app.** Help > How the numbers are calculated (`electrochem/methods_text.py`: twelve topics in plain words and in full, the open file's settings filled in, a methods paragraph to copy; tied to the code by `tests/test_methods_text.py`); Help > Cite Cyclara (`electrochem/citation.py`: reference, BibTeX, CITATION.cff, methods sentence; `python -m electrochem.analyze --cite`; the committed `CITATION.cff` must equal the generated one); `settings.json` carries the methods paragraph and the citation.

## LANHE `.cex` and a step that ended at once (0.9.0, 21 October 2026)

- **LANHE `.cex` read natively (P08g; implemented and software-tested; verified on one real file, a user's and private, by four statements inside it; LANHE's own export of that file not yet compared).** `electrochem/readers/lanhe_cex.py`: layout `07 00 04 00`, 0.01 s time unit, 5 V and 1 mA over 32 256 counts, the tester's float32 capacity and energy counters. Each file opened is checked where it holds enough records: record time against the tester's clock (two step starts at least 60 s apart), decoded current against the capacity counter and decoded voltage against the energy counter (at least 20 intervals; within 0.2 % on constant-current steps, 1 % when only constant-voltage steps qualify; the voltage only where it stays positive); a note says when a check could not be made. Every file's step currents are checked against their step types, and its counters and framing are checked. A layout, step type, status event or unit kind not seen before, or a range code the file's counters cannot confirm, is refused with what was found; block types not seen before and retention-reference units are skipped with a note. The tester's step table is exported as `instrument_steps.csv` (start times on the tester's clock, written without a time zone because the file records none); the reader and its checks are in `settings.json`; the app answers the current direction from the step types and shows why. `docs/P08g_LANHE_cex_Reader.md`; `tests/test_readers_cex.py` (29 tests on synthetic files written by `tests/cex_synthetic.py`; 28 deliberate faults put into the reader, all caught).
- **A step that ended at once (M01 section 6, amended).** Found while checking a real file's outputs: a step that ended at its first check, paired charge-first with the full branch after it, gave a coulombic efficiency of millions of per cent. A pair in which one branch has at most two rows and under 0.1 % of the other's charge now keeps the capacities, energies and potentials of both branches but has no efficiency, energy efficiency or hysteresis, with the reason in its note. When that branch is the discharge, the cycle has no retention either and stays out of the cycle reaching 80 %, the fade line, the mean discharge potential and the first-cycle utilisation; when it is the reference cycle's discharge, no retention is given. The app asks the cycle order when a file opens with such a branch. `tests/test_degenerate_pairs.py` (19 tests; 22 deliberate faults in the rule, the figure fixes, the methods text and the app wording, all caught).
- **Independent review before the release (21 October 2026).** A separate agent compared every statement about the two items above with the code. It found a defect, fixed with a test: Help > How the numbers are calculated failed on its Energy topic for a file whose first cycle has a withheld hysteresis. It found that a discharge that ended at once still gave a retention, which then set the cycle reaching 80 % (fixed, M01 section 6); that the `.cex` voltage and clock checks were skipped without a note when a file could not support them (notes added); that the step table called the tester's clock UTC though the file records no time zone (the column is now `start_clock`); and wording that claimed more than the code does (a check on every file, a refusal for everything not seen before, a remedy in every refusal), corrected in the README, the handout, `HOW_TO_USE.txt`, this file, P08g, P08d, M01 and the release notes.
- **Figures and app wording found on the way.** The dQ/dE map's panels share their cycle rows (the charge panel showed nothing when its curves began a cycle later); a single cycle is labelled with its own number, not 1.50, 1.75, ...; the summary's bin-width note no longer sits on a curve; figures and the app draw a cycle's own hysteresis (withheld stays withheld) and drop empty legend entries; diagnostics, the cycle strip and the capacity view tell a cut-short cycle from an unpaired one; the methods window names the profile a file was read with (it never did); the command line no longer stops when a mean hysteresis is withheld.
- **Unreadable files refused in words.** The `.cex` first reached the window as a raw `csv` error. `.res`, `.nda` and `.ndax` stay refused with an instruction to export text; any other file with NUL bytes is refused as `binary_content` before layout detection; the detection ends a row at a lone carriage return and honours UTF-16 and UTF-32 byte-order marks, so Excel's "Unicode text" export opens. Tests on synthetic files: `tests/test_readers_unreadable.py`.
- **Reproducible launcher.** Two builds of the same sources give the same `Cyclara.exe` (`tests/test_installer.py`).

## LANHE pauses and restarts, and the dQ/dE of an interrupted branch (0.9.1, 21 October 2026)

- **A second real `.cex` read (P08g section 8; implemented and software-tested; verified on that file, a user's and private, from the records on both sides of every event and the tester's clock; LANHE's own export not yet compared).** 0.9.0 refused it, as designed: layout `05 00 04 00` and status events it had not seen. Reader `lanhe-cex` 1.1.0 reads the layout (the same units, records and scales as `07 00 04 00`, its checks passing to 0.0002 % on the current and 0.00001 % on the voltage) and the events: pauses (the record time does not count them; inside a step the records must continue across each; the clock check adds them back), stops after which the tester restored its state of a few seconds earlier (every record kept, both passes of the replayed seconds included; the charge the tester's counter left out kept; the interval across the restart not integrated, so the branch is partial, with a warning at each), the resume details (checked for their place) and five other event codes (checked against the records inside a step, listed elsewhere). Events out of the order seen, a restart unlike the ones seen, and an event or pause where the records break are refused with the reason. The time unit is judged interval by interval, so one change of the tester's clock no longer moves the test.
- **No dQ/dE for a branch with a curve break or a pause (M01 section 9.3, amended).** Found on that file: the cell relaxed while the tester was stopped, and each interrupted branch showed a large false dQ/dE peak. A branch with an interval where the time goes back, a current is missing or an error flag is set, or one that a LANHE tester paused partway (the potential relaxes while the current is off; by a few millivolts at the pauses of that file), now has no dQ/dE, and its cycle's note says which branch and why; a pause leaves the branch's capacity and its cycle's efficiency and retention as they are. Only an interruption between the first and last row of the curve (outside constant-voltage stages) counts, and for pauses the rule is a precaution (the curves at that file's pauses did not differ visibly); the figures, views and peak tables leave such a branch out, and a figure without any curve says why. A census of the real files in `data/public` caught the first form of the rule, which counted zero-duration intervals and would have removed every dQ/dE curve of the Landt Excel example (its time is printed to 0.01 h); with the rule as kept, every public real file gives the cycle values, curves and summary values of 0.9.0. The census also showed that no cycle of that Landt export has an efficiency, because most of its rows share a time and M01 section 5.1 integrates no interval without advancing time; this has been so since P08d, is now written in P08d and in each cycle's note, and is an open question in M01 section 5.1.
- **The summary** names the incomplete cycles it leaves out (the first twelve, and how many in all), not only an unfinished last one, and says when the reference cycle is not complete, so that no cycle has a retention (M01 section 6, amended); so does the methods paragraph.
- **Independent review before the build.** A separate agent compared every statement about the work with the code and tried edge cases. Of its 18 findings, the substantive ones were a restart going back 0 s being accepted, pauses folding the potential like stops, a restart in the reference cycle removing every retention unannounced, a valid short step refused, and pauses without a continuity check; the rest were wording that claimed more checking than the code did. All are fixed, each listed in `docs/handoffs/Project_Status_Addendum_2026-10-21b.md`. A second agent then reviewed the fixes: of its eleven findings, the substantive ones were an export that stopped at the 3D figure when no branch had a dQ/dE curve (an error since 0.8.0), a peak whose partner branch had no curve reported as having no partner branch, a pause or restart in a constant-voltage hold needlessly removing a curve, a short rest with an event still refused, and the energy counter not checked at a restart; the rest were wording. All are fixed, with tests, and listed in the same addendum. A third agent reviewed those fixes and found the methods paragraph silent on an interruption inside a constant-voltage hold, the reader's messages not yet following the hold rule, a stale note in the dQ/dE view and some wording; a build made before its report was discarded, and the findings were fixed, with tests (same addendum). A fourth agent then found the methods paragraph contradicting itself for a branch both paused in its curve and restarted in its hold, the same stale note in the Evolution view, and wording; a second build was discarded and those were fixed too. A fifth agent, reviewing those fixes, found nothing that blocked the release but seven points (an edge case in the restart warning, untested note clearings, wording); a third build was stopped to take them in.
- **Tests and evidence.** `tests/test_readers_cex.py` (72 tests), `tests/test_interrupted_branches.py` (7), `tests/test_segment_speed.py` (the vectorised count of curve breaks and their reasons against a row-by-row form), `tests/test_plateaus.py`, `tests/test_evolution_view.py` and `tests/test_studio.py` (branches without a curve in the peak tables and the views); 94 deliberate faults in the new code, 93 caught, the other equivalent to the code (`docs/evidence/p08g_cex_1.1.0/`). The private regression checks of both real files pass: the first file's readings and results unchanged digit for digit, the second's as established by hand before the reader read it; the author's own EC-Lab file gives the same results as under 0.9.0.

## Fixed conventions

- Original bytes, normalised observations and derived results stay separate. Source hashes, row references, flags, revisions and processing settings are preserved.
- Never infer mass, area, current direction, reference electrode, cycle order or geometry. `Ewe` is not terminal voltage until confirmed. EC-Lab's empty-field mass default (0.001 mg) is never offered.
- Integration only over supported edges. Time resets, duplicate timestamps, missing current, error flags and current-sign changes across a programmed boundary are unsupported and reported with a reason. A programmed boundary with advancing time and unchanged direction is included. Without a step (Ns) column, a branch ends wherever the measured current changes sign. A branch whose time goes back, whose current is missing, which the instrument flagged, or which a LANHE tester paused partway has no dQ/dE; a zero-duration interval does not stop it.
- Direction is decided per programmed step from the net measured current, because the first row of a step still carries the previous step's ox/red flag; the flag is a cross-check and the fallback.
- The instrument's per-row dq increment is the primary charge when present (full-rate integration); the trapezoid of sampled current is reported beside it. On constant-current stages they agree to about 1e-6; on sparsely sampled constant-voltage tails the trapezoid overstates the tail, which is why dq is preferred.
- Coulombic efficiency is Q_discharge / Q_charge of the same cycle, never clipped, whichever branch starts the cycle.
- Energy and mean voltage need the potential's meaning (`--terminal-voltage` or `--reference`). Named charge/discharge, efficiency and retention need `--charge`. Neither is inferred from labels: the native reader labels the same field `Ewe/V` that the text export calls `Ecell/V`.
- A half-cell answer analyses the working-electrode column (Ewe/V) and a full-cell answer the cell voltage where a file holds both.
- Pydantic 2.13.5 for validation; Galvani revision d4a5f44 plus NumPy 2.3.5 for the optional native path, with source fingerprint and version gates.
- Unknown uncertainty is not zero. Cycles are not independent replicates.

## Known limits

- Size limits. The app and `python -m electrochem.analyze` read EC-Lab text exports of up to 2 GiB through the fast path and `.mpr` files with no size cap of their own (memory is the limit). A file the fast path hands to the strict importer (anything unusual) is held to that importer's limits: 256 MiB, and for `.mpr` 5,000,000 records, 128 fields and one data module; the strict text importer accepts declared profiles only. Other cyclers' exports: up to 2 GiB. Snapshots: 50 MiB uncompressed.
- Native versus text comparison across six public files shows 415 last-digit differences: 362 round-trip to the same binary value, 53 differ by at most four ULP after decimal parsing. This is export rounding, not an import defect. The native record is treated as the primary source where both exist.
- The one-click installers are tested under Wine (Windows) and by static checks (macOS), not yet by a user on a physical Windows PC or a Mac, and they are not code-signed, so Windows and macOS ask once before the first launch. No screen-reader test.
- LANHE `.cex`: two layouts and one pair of range codes verified, on two real files of one user; neither compared yet with LANHE's own Excel export. A branch that reaches the end of a file counts as interrupted even when the tester wrote its end-of-test event (M01), so a test that ends in its last step has no efficiency for its last cycle. A cycle with a restart has no efficiency or retention, though its capacities include every recorded increment; a pause leaves capacities, efficiency and retention as they are, but a branch paused partway has no dQ/dE, even after a pause of a few seconds (a precaution), unless the pause falls in a constant-voltage hold at the start or end of the branch.
- The Landt Excel export checked (P08d) prints the time to 0.01 h, so most consecutive rows share a time; M01 integrates no interval without advancing time, so no cycle of it has an efficiency or retention (capacities and dQ/dE are given). A rule for times shared only through print resolution is an open M01 question.
- The CV desktop app prototype (P12a, delivered separately; not in `main` or the installers) may carry the pyqtgraph SI-prefix defect found in Cyclara (U02, section 10); run the same test on it before it is merged.
