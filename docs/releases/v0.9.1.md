# Cyclara 0.9.1

Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later) that turns battery cycling data into checked numbers and journal-ready figures. It reads BioLogic EC-Lab `.mpr` and `.mpt` files, LANHE (LAND) `.cex` files, and the text or Excel exports of Arbin, Maccor, Neware, BaSyTec, Novonix, PEC and Landt cyclers, and learns any other cycler's export from one file.

## New in 0.9.1

- **LANHE `.cex` files with pauses and restarts open.** 0.9.0 refused the second `.cex` layout seen (`05 00 04 00`) and any test that had been paused or stopped. 0.9.1 reads both. A pause is read as it is, and the check of the record time against the tester's clock allows for it. When the tester stopped and then resumed from a state it had saved shortly before, it records those seconds twice and its own capacity counter drops their charge: Cyclara keeps every record and that charge, warns at each restart, and treats the interrupted branch as partial, so its cycle has no efficiency or retention (and no cycle has a retention when that cycle is the reference). Events in an order not seen before, a restart unlike the ones seen, and a place where the records break are refused with the reason. Verified on a second real file; a comparison with LANHE's own Excel export is still to come.
- **No dQ/dE across an interruption.** A branch in which the time goes back, the current is missing or the instrument flagged an error has no dQ/dE, and neither has a branch a LANHE tester paused partway: the potential after the break need not continue the curve before it, and such curves showed false peaks. A paused branch keeps its capacities, efficiency and retention. Rows that only share a time stamp are not a break. The cycle's note says which branch has no dQ/dE and why.
- **The summary names the cycles it leaves out**, not only an unfinished last one, and says when the reference cycle is not complete, so that no cycle has a retention.
- The record time of a `.cex` is checked against the tester's clock interval by interval, so one change of the tester's clock (a summer-time change, for example) is reported with its place instead of refusing the file.
- Known, and unchanged from earlier versions: Landt Excel exports print the time to 0.01 h, so most consecutive rows share a time, and no cycle of such an export gets an efficiency or a retention (capacities and dQ/dE are given). Each cycle's note now says how many intervals were not integrated.

## Install

**Windows** 10 (version 1903 or later) or 11, 64-bit: run `Cyclara-0.9.1-Setup.exe`, then Next, Install, Finish. No administrator password is needed, and it installs over 0.9.0. The first time, SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer is not signed with a paid certificate: click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.

**macOS** 14 or later: unzip `Cyclara-0.9.1-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip` (M1 to M4) or `Cyclara-0.9.1-macOS-Intel.zip`, drag Cyclara.app to Applications and open it. The first time, macOS says it cannot verify the app: click Done, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > *Open Anyway*, and open it again. On macOS 14, right-click the app and choose Open.

The handout (`Cyclara_handout_v0.9.1.pdf`) shows every step with pictures.
