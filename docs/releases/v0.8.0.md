# Cyclara 0.8.0

Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later) that turns battery cycling data into checked numbers and journal-ready figures. It reads BioLogic EC-Lab `.mpr` and `.mpt` files and the text or Excel exports of Arbin, Maccor, Neware, BaSyTec, Novonix, PEC and Landt cyclers, and learns any other cycler's export from one file.

## New in 0.8.0

- **Several plateaus.** Every dQ/dE peak gets the capacity under it, its partner in the opposite branch of the same cycle (within 200 mV) or the status *unpaired*, and the cycles in which it is seen (*first cycle only*, *appears from cycle k*). Nothing is labelled reversible or irreversible: that judgement stays with you. The average potential of a branch is the capacity-weighted mean of its plateau potentials.
- **Evolution view and two new figures**: dQ/dE of every cycle as a colour map, and as 3D surfaces (charge and discharge).
- **Large files**: a million-row EC-Lab export opens in a few seconds; other cyclers' exports are read in chunks with the progress shown, up to 2 GB.
- **Help > How the numbers are calculated**: every quantity in plain words and in full, with the settings of the open file and a methods paragraph to copy.
- **Help > Cite Cyclara**: the reference, BibTeX, CITATION.cff or a methods sentence, one click each.
- Fixed: files over 100 000 rows raised an error in the time plots; the average voltage was described as energy-weighted (it is charge-weighted; the numbers were always right).

## Install

**Windows** 10 (version 1903 or later) or 11, 64-bit: run `Cyclara-0.8.0-Setup.exe`, then Next, Install, Finish. No administrator password is needed, and it installs over 0.7.0. The first time, SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer is not signed with a paid certificate: click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.

**macOS** 14 or later: unzip `Cyclara-0.8.0-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip` (M1 to M4) or `Cyclara-0.8.0-macOS-Intel.zip`, drag Cyclara.app to Applications and open it. The first time, macOS says it cannot verify the app: click Done, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > *Open Anyway*, and open it again. On macOS 14, right-click the app and choose Open.

The handout (`Cyclara_handout_v0.8.0.pdf`) shows every step with pictures.
